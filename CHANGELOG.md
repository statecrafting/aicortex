# Changelog

Releases of the aicortex library crates (`aicortex-types`,
`aicortex-claims`, `aicortex-gate`, `aicortex-store`, `aicortex-index`, `aicortex-embed`).
`docs/release.md` is the procedure and the section format. A release is a
publication of library crates; no section here describes a deployment, an
image, a reference-deployment qualification, or production readiness.

## Unreleased

No release has been cut. The crates are publishable from an annotated tag a
human pushes (spec 055); until then a host-library consumer (spec 053) pins
an exact source revision.
